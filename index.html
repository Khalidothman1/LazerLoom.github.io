Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 2, 2026  ·  Version 1.0

// No personal data collected // Ads may collect data // GDPR friendly

01 Overview // intro

Welcome to Laser Loom — a neon-themed laser beam puzzle game. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you play our game on Android.

// SHORT VERSION: We do not collect, store, or share any personal information about you. The only data activity comes from third-party advertising (Google AdMob), which operates under its own privacy framework.

By playing Laser Loom, you agree to the terms described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

02 Data We Collect // our_data

We collect absolutely nothing. Laser Loom does not collect, transmit, store, or process any personal data from its users. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no backend servers operated by us.

✗ No name ✗ No email ✗ No location ✗ No device ID ✗ No usage analytics ✗ No crash logs ✗ No contacts ✗ No camera / microphone

All game progress (such as level completion and scores) is stored locally on your device only using your device's storage. This data never leaves your device.

03 Advertising (Google AdMob) // ads_data

Laser Loom displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob is a third-party advertising network operated by Google LLC. When ads are displayed, AdMob may collect certain data from your device to serve relevant advertisements.

// Data AdMob may collect includes: advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information, and interaction data with ads. This data is governed solely by Google's Privacy Policy.

This data is collected by Google AdMob directly and is subject to Google's own Privacy Policy, which you can review at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings:

Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization

04 Third-Party Services // third_party

The only third-party service integrated into Laser Loom is Google AdMob for advertising. No other analytics, tracking, or data-collection SDKs are included in the app.

The following links detail the privacy practices of our third-party partner:

· Google AdMob — AdMob Privacy & Terms
· Google Privacy Policy — policies.google.com/privacy
· Google Families Policy — Families Policy

05 Children's Privacy // coppa

Laser Loom is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since we collect no personal data at all, there is no risk of inadvertent collection from minors through our own systems.

However, please be aware that Google AdMob's data practices apply as described in Section 03. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has been exposed to advertising data collection, please review Google's Family Controls at the link provided above.

06 Data Security // security

Since Laser Loom does not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no personal data at risk from our systems. All game data lives locally on your device and is protected by your device's own security measures.

For data handled by Google AdMob, Google employs industry-standard security practices. For more details, refer to Google's security documentation.

07 Your Rights // gdpr_ccpa

Since we collect no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export, or delete on your behalf. You retain full control over your local game data — simply uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data from your device.

For rights related to advertising data collected by Google AdMob (including GDPR or CCPA requests), please contact Google directly through their privacy tools at myaccount.google.com.

08 Changes to This Policy // updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of Laser Loom after any changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the revised terms.
